Zakat Calculator

زكاة · a zakat year from the day you choose · everything stays on this device

How it works. Pick the day your zakat year starts and what you held that day. Add what you gain each month and any amounts on particular dates. Record the zakat you pay. For every completed zakat year the calculator shows the wealth held at its end, whether it was above the nisab, the zakat that was due (2.5 %), what you paid for that year, and whether you met it, paid above it or fell below it. The year in progress is projected.

A zakat year is a lunar year (354 days). Payments made within a grace period after a year ends count for that year; earlier ones count as advance payments for the year in progress. Overpayment carries forward. Nothing here is sent anywhere — there is no server.
