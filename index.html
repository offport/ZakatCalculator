Zakat Calculator

زكاة · a zakat year from the day your wealth reaches the nisab · everything stays on this device

How it works. Pick the day you want to count from and what you held that day. The zakat year (hawl) begins on the first day that wealth is at or above the nisab and runs one lunar year. Add what you gain each month, amounts on particular dates, and debts that are due. Record the zakat you pay. For every completed year the calculator shows the wealth at its end (after debts), whether it was above the nisab at that year's gold or silver price, the zakat due (2.5 %), what was paid for it, and whether you met it, paid above it or are still below it. The year in progress is projected.

Assumptions. Hanafi rule by default: only the wealth at the year's end is assessed, and a dip below the nisab during the year is noted but does not break the year (the Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali view, where a dip restarts the year, is a switch in the settings). A payment settles the oldest unpaid year first. An overpayment carries forward only if it was paid with the intention of zakat — otherwise it is ordinary charity. Nothing here is sent anywhere — there is no server.

2Monthly gains

What you add to your wealth each month — salary savings, rent, profit. Accrues on the same day every month from the date you give.

    3Amounts on a date

    A bonus, an inheritance, a sale — or money that left: enter a negative amount.

      4Debts due

      Money you owe that is due — a loan instalment, a bill, rent owed. It is deducted from the wealth while it is outstanding; give the date it was settled to stop deducting it. Long-term debt is usually only deducted for the instalments due within the year.

        5Zakat paid

        Each payment is matched to a zakat year automatically and settles the oldest unpaid year first; choose a year yourself if it should count for a different one.
